Operator field card

Getting paid, the season, and what customers can change themselves.

The one rule

Always tap Done when the bin is clean — that's what charges the card, sends the customer their photos, and closes the job. Choose how they're paying before you tap it.

The four ways to get paid

1
💳 Card on file

Most jobs. They saved a card when they booked.

  1. Nothing to pick — this is already selected.
  2. Tap Done. It charges automatically.
They get: their before/after animation and a receipt. Badge turns 💳 paid
2
💵 Cash

They hand you cash at the door.

  1. Tap Cash.
  2. Check the amount — it fills in for you ($45 first bin, $12 each extra). Type over it if you agreed on something different.
  3. Tap Done.
They get: a receipt that correctly says paid in cash — it never claims you charged a card. Badge turns 💵 cash
3
📱 QR code

They want to pay by card but have no card on file. Fastest option — no card handling.

  1. Tap QR, then tap Done.
  2. A QR code appears at the top of your screen. Hold your phone up.
  3. They scan it and pay on their phone — Apple Pay, Google Pay, or card.
  4. Once they've paid, tap the × to dismiss the code.
Job shows ⏳ awaiting payment until they actually pay, then flips to paid and their receipt sends itself.
4
🔖 Tap in Stripe

Backup — their physical card, tapped on your phone. Use when the QR won't work for them.

  1. Tap Tap in Stripe — it opens the Stripe app.
  2. Type the amount in the Stripe app yourself, then take the tap there.
  3. Come back to Ops and tap Done to record it.
This one doesn't link up on its own — the money lands in Stripe, and tapping Done here is what keeps the books straight. Badge turns 🔖 tapped

Someone flags you down

+ New job here

Button at the top of Today. Takes about twenty seconds.

  • Enter the street, postal code, and how many bins.
  • Ask for their email if they'll give it — that's how they get their receipt and photos. Skip it if they'd rather not; cash still works fine.
  • Tap Start job. It appears in Today like any other stop — photos, Done, payment, all the same.

Watch for these

Red "⚠ Nothing collected" after you tap Done

You finished the job but no money was taken — usually a walk-up with no card where Cash or QR wasn't picked. Go back and settle it before you drive off.

They give up on the QR and pay cash instead

The QR link stays live for a while. Tell them not to scan it later, or they'll pay twice.

Before photo, every time

Step 1 must go green with a thumbnail before you tap Done. No before photo means no leprechaun wash animation in their email — just a plain photo.

The season

May 1 → October 31

You can't pressure-wash a bin in an Alberta winter, so the whole system knows to stop. You don't have to remember any of this — but customers will ask, so here's the short version.

  • Nothing gets booked past October 31. A monthly plan only ever schedules the cleans that fit before the season closes. Same for someone booking in September — they might only get one.
  • Nobody is charged over the winter. Money only moves when you tap Done on a real clean, so a paused plan costs them nothing.
  • Their plan doesn't cancel. On their booking page they see "Paused for winter" and the month we're back. If someone worries they've been cut off, that's the answer.

Starting up in the spring

Nothing books itself in May. When the ground has thawed and you're ready to run:

  • Open the History tab and tap Open the new season.
  • It books the new season for every active plan and emails those customers their first date.
  • Tapping it twice is fine — it won't double-book anyone or send a second email. If you're not sure it worked, just tap it again and read what it says.

They want a different day

Customers can move their own cleans now

On their booking page every upcoming clean has a Change date button, so you don't have to sort this out for them. Point them at the manage link in any of their emails.

  • It moves only that one clean — the rest of their schedule stays where it is.
  • It won't let them pick a Sunday, a date in the past, or anything outside the season.
  • They can still Skip a single clean or cancel a one-off, same as before.

Chasing money later

The Needs attention tab is your money to-do list. It shows every finished job that still owes, with the amount:

  • ⚠ card failed — their saved card declined. Retry it there, or collect another way.
  • ⏳ waiting on payment — a QR they never scanned.
  • ⚠ not collected — the job was finished without taking anything.
